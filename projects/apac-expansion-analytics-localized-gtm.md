# APAC Expansion — Analytics Modernization & Localized GTM

> **Portfolio lens:** Transformation strategy, market entry, target operating model, data governance, localization, and value capture.

## Executive snapshot

Led a two-year transformation supporting a $1.1B APAC opportunity. A governed analytics platform became the decision system for a hub-and-spoke market-entry model, exposing regional lead bias and translating local regulation into data, messaging, approval, and sales rules.

## Resume-ready impact

- Led transformation from three-week reporting lag to sub-15-minute decision data while reducing total cost of ownership 40%.
- Defined platform ownership, data stewardship, certified metrics, regional challenge rights, and a hub-and-spoke operating model for market entry.
- Improved conversion from 4% to 22%, reduced CAC payback from 18 to nine months, shortened compliance review 30%, and enabled $1.1B in APAC AUM.

## Interview story

### Situation

The expansion strategy relied on fragmented reports, unowned data quality, and a copied market playbook that converted only 8% in Asia.

### Task

Build the decision and operating system required for compliant regional scale, not merely modernize technology.

### Actions

- Established explicit reliability, quality, metric, and regional-ownership roles.
- Proved value with a single real-time launch dashboard before funding the full lakehouse.
- Used local experts to identify bias in the global scoring model and recalibrate by market.
- Designed Singapore governance with local data and go-to-market spokes in Vietnam and Indonesia.

### Results

- Data latency fell below 15 minutes, and TCO declined 40%.
- Conversion reached 22%.
- CAC payback fell to nine months, and compliance review time improved 30%.
- The expansion supported $1.1B in APAC AUM.

## Decisions and trade-offs

- Create accountable data ownership before scaling market activity.
- Keep primary regulated data in-country while returning anonymized insight to the regional hub.
- Turn compliance into a repeatable sales and operating guide rather than case-by-case review.

## Leadership signal

Aligned technology, analytics, marketing, sales, compliance, finance, and regional leaders around one market-entry scorecard and a target operating model.
