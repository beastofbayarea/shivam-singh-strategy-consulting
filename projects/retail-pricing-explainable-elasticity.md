# Retail Pricing — Explainable Elasticity & Merchant Adoption

> **Portfolio lens:** Pricing strategy, causal experimentation, portfolio economics, operating-model redesign, and change adoption.

## Executive snapshot

Designed an elasticity-based pricing system that replaced uniform cost-plus decisions with a continuous, explainable portfolio loop. Randomized tests created the variation missing from history; SKU roles, guardrails, merchant reasoning, and cross-functional pods translated the model into accepted action.

## Resume-ready impact

- Led pricing strategy that unlocked an estimated $50M revenue opportunity with 95% forecast accuracy.
- Introduced randomized store-level price tests and interpretable elasticity modeling where static historical prices could not reveal causal demand response.
- Reduced local price-change execution from six weeks to under one and lowered merchant overrides below 5% by month two.

## Interview story

### Situation

Essentials were overpriced, niche items left margin unused, and historical data had too little price movement to support credible elasticity estimates. Merchant distrust nearly stopped the pilot.

### Task

Create a pricing and operating system that balanced customer value perception, basket economics, margin, learning, and merchant judgment.

### Actions

- Designed randomized, stratified tests with A/A checks, power calculations, staggered windows, difference-in-differences, and spillover controls.
- Assigned products roles such as key-value item, basket builder, core, long tail, and heritage item.
- Added plain-language rationale, confidence, history, price-ladder constraints, and reason-coded overrides to each recommendation.
- Reorganized merchant, data science, and marketing work into weekly outcome pods.

### Results

- The program identified an estimated $50M revenue opportunity.
- Forecast accuracy reached 95%.
- Price-change execution fell below one week.
- Overrides declined below 5% by month two.

## Decisions and trade-offs

- Create causal variation before optimizing from historical data.
- Optimize portfolio and basket economics rather than every SKU independently.
- Protect top visible items and cap weekly price movement at plus or minus 5%.

## Leadership signal

Converted a black-box model into a glass-box operating system that gave merchants evidence, agency, and accountable override while preserving customer and brand guardrails.
